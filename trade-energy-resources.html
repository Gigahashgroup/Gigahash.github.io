Division overview

Energy leadership. Clear sectors.

Oil, gas and petroleum form a core commercial focus within our largest division, alongside mining, minerals and selected specialty commodities.

Core business

Oil, Gas & Petroleum

A dedicated commercial pathway.

Gigahash evaluates qualified mandates involving crude oil, natural gas, bitumen and refined petroleum products. Our role is to develop credible sourcing and supply relationships, establish counterparty fit and coordinate a disciplined path toward diligence and execution.

We do not present a public spot inventory. Product availability, specifications, volumes, geography and transaction requirements are addressed through qualified, mandate-led conversations.
