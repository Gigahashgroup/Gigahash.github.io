Division overview

Broad capability. Clear sectors.

Our largest division is organized around three focused sectors. This keeps each conversation relevant while allowing Gigahash to connect opportunities that cross supply, logistics, technology, development and market access.

Specific products and projects are discussed through qualified inquiries rather than published as a generic inventory. Every conversation begins with the mandate, geography, commercial readiness and counterparty requirements.

Sector architecture

Three focused commercial pathways.

We organize opportunities by sector so that diligence, expertise and counterparties remain relevant to the mandate.

Energy & petroleum

Sourcing and commercial relationships across crude oil, natural gas, bitumen, aviation and automotive fuels, and other refined products.

Mining & minerals

Gold and other mineral prospects, project development, mineral sourcing, and strategic or energy-transition minerals.
