Privacy Policy

Effective August 21, 2026

This policy describes the information practices of the Gigahash Global corporate website. Separate products, including a future RewardsPlanet application, may require additional product-specific privacy notices before launch.

Information we collect

We collect information that you voluntarily provide through our contact and opportunity forms, including your name, email address, organization, telephone number, region, inquiry type and message.

Our form service provider may also process technical information necessary to deliver and protect submissions, such as IP address, browser information and anti-abuse signals.

How we use information

  • To review and respond to inquiries.
  • To route opportunities to the appropriate Gigahash business area.
  • To protect the website and forms against misuse.
  • To maintain appropriate business and compliance records.

Service providers

Website form submissions are processed through Formspree. Information submitted through a form is subject to Formspree's processing practices as well as this policy. Production images are served directly from the Gigahash Global website.

Cookies and analytics

This version of the website does not intentionally set advertising cookies or operate a configured analytics service. If analytics or additional tracking technologies are introduced, this policy and any required consent controls should be updated before activation.

Sharing and retention

Gigahash does not sell personal information submitted through this website. Information may be shared with service providers, advisers or prospective project participants when reasonably necessary to evaluate or respond to an inquiry and subject to appropriate obligations. We retain information only as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose collected, legal obligations and legitimate business records.

Your choices

You may request access, correction or deletion of information you submitted, subject to legal and operational requirements. Do not submit confidential, export-controlled, financial-account or highly sensitive personal information through the public website.

Security and international processing

We use reasonable administrative and technical measures appropriate to a static corporate website. No internet transmission is completely secure. Information may be processed in the United States or other jurisdictions where Gigahash or its service providers operate.

Contact

Questions or privacy requests may be sent to info@gigahashgroup.com or Gigahash Group LLC, 2226 Eastlake Ave E, #425, Seattle, WA 98102, United States.

This policy should receive appropriate legal review and be reviewed again before analytics, accounts, payments, competitions or RewardsPlanet functionality is activated.
