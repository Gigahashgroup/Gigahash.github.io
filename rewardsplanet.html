The concept

Participation should feel rewarding.

RewardsPlanet explores a more engaging relationship between brands, consumers and communities. The venture is being shaped around sponsored participation, competitive experiences and rewards that people can value.

It sits within the Gigahash portfolio as a distinct consumer-facing venture while benefiting from the parent company's technology and partnership capabilities. As the application develops, this page can expand into the full product website with participation guides, sponsor options, sign-in and app launch access.
